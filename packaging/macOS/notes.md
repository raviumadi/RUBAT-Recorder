## Summary

Multichannel audio recorder with live heterodyne monitoring, dual spectrograms, and flexible recording workflows for bioacoustics and beyond.

---

## Description

RUBAT Studio (Realtime Unified BioAcoustic Tool) is a standalone multichannel audio recording application. It supports sample rates up to 500 kHz, live heterodyne and passthrough monitoring, dual spectrogram display, and three recording modes — Tap, Continuous, and Auto (threshold-triggered). Designed for ultrasonic field bioacoustics (bat surveys, insect acoustics, bird vocalisations) but equally suited for any multichannel recording scenario.

Key features:
• Up to 64 input/output channels with per-channel selection
• Real-time heterodyne frequency shifting with phase-continuous carrier
• Dual independent spectrograms and scrolling dBFS/dB SPL waveform display
• Ring-buffer pre-trigger capture across all recording modes
• Auto-mode with configurable threshold, peak count, and cooldown
• Calibration support for absolute dB SPL readings
• Broadcast WAV output with timestamped filenames and embedded metadata

Requires MATLAB Runtime R2023b or later (included with installer).
